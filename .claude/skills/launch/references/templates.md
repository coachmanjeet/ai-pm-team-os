# Launch Templates

```markdown
# [Feature Name] is Now GA!

**Release:** [4.2] | **GA Date:** [Date] | **Team:** [Scrum Team]

## What's Launching
[2-3 sentence description of the feature and its value]

## Key Capabilities
- **[Capability 1]:** [Brief description]
- **[Capability 2]:** [Brief description]

## Who It's For
- [Persona 1] — [How it helps them]
- [Persona 2] — [How it helps them]

## How to Enable
[Step-by-step or link to setup guide]

## Resources
- Help Documentation | Demo Video | Release Notes

## Known Limitations
- [Limitation 1]

## Team Shoutouts
Engineering: [Names] | UX: [Names] | QA: [Names]
```
