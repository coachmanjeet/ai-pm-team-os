| Folder | You Own | What to Do |

## Where Design Lives

Primary design artifacts live in **Figma** — link them from PRDs and specs. Use the `product-development/design/` folder for supplementary documents:
- Design principles and guidelines
- Accessibility standards
- UX research synthesis
- Design system documentation

## Key References

- `context/WRITING-GUIDE.md` — Voice and tone guidelines

## Contribution Workflow

1. Link Figma files in PRDs and feature-index.yaml
2. Review PRDs from the UX perspective
3. Store UX research summaries in `product-development/design/`
4. Use `/share-update` to notify the team
