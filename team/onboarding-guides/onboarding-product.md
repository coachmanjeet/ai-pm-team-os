# Onboarding — Product Managers

Welcome! This guide supplements the [general onboarding](onboarding-general.md) with PM-specific setup.

## Your Key Folders

| Folder | You Own | What to Do |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| `product-development/product/PRDs/` | Yes | Write PRDs here, organized by product area |
| `product-development/product/customers/` | Co-own | Log customer calls with `/customer-call` |
| `product-development/product/strategy/` | Yes | Store roadmaps, vision, strategy memos |
| `product-development/product/launches/` | Yes | Create launch materials with `/launch` |
| `context/` | Yes | Keep domain knowledge current |
| `tracker/` | Co-own | Log ideas, decisions |

## Essential Skills

| Skill | Learn First | When |
|-------|------------|------|
| `/prd` | Day 1 | Creating PRDs |
| `/customer-call` | Day 1 | After every customer call |
| `/workflow feature [topic]` | Week 1 | End-to-end feature development |
| `/multi-agent-review [doc]` | Week 1 | Getting feedback on PRDs |
| `/competitive-analysis` | Week 2 | Competitive positioning |
| `/strategy` | Week 2 | Strategy memos |

## Key Processes

1. **Feature work:** Use `/workflow feature` for the full flow (Research → PRD → Review → QA → Stories)
2. **Launch readiness:** Repo must be updated before launch (see `.claude/rules/launch-readiness.md`)
3. **Decisions:** Track all decisions in `tracker/decisions.md`
4. **Plans:** Save complex plans to `plans/` for future reference
