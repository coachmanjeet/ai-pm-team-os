# Meetings — Structured Meeting Notes

Meeting summaries with transcripts. Each meeting type has `docs/` (raw notes) and `summaries/` (structured summaries).

| Folder | Cadence | What's Captured |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| `sprint-planning/` | Per sprint | Sprint goals, committed work, capacity |
| `standup/` | Daily/async | Blockers, progress, needs |
| `team-syncs/` | Weekly/bi-weekly | Cross-functional alignment, decisions |

**Structure per meeting type:**
```
[meeting-type]/
  docs/YYYY-MM-DD-[topic].md        — Raw meeting notes
  summaries/YYYY-MM-DD.md           — Structured summary
```
